\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

\textbf{Benchmarks.} (i) \emph{robomimic} can and square (state-based,
300-episode evaluations, last-3-checkpoint average, 3 seeds), run in the
official DICE-RL harness with our actor update implanted --- critic, replay,
demo data, and evaluation protocol byte-identical to the baseline; (ii) the
\emph{eight hardest LIBERO-90 tasks} (multitask base, 100 RL iterations,
20-episode checkpoint evals with $\pm 11$pp noise; final numbers use powered
evaluation, 100 rollouts $\times$ 3 evaluation seeds). \todo{exact env,
horizon, demo counts table in appendix.}

\textbf{Arms.} All methods share the drifting base, residual
parameterization, critic, and data pipeline; only the actor update differs.
\textbf{A} = DICE-RL residual actor on the drift base (backpropagated
$-Q$ + filtered BC anchor; the control). \textbf{B} = \method{}
(\cref{eq:update} with $V_Q^{\mathrm{grad}}$). \textbf{C/T} = the
zeroth-order top-$k$ / tilted reward-field dial settings. \textbf{FM+DICE}
= the unmodified state-of-the-art pipeline on the flow-matching base.
Hyperparameters are \emph{family-shared} across B/C/T ($\eta_Q{=}0.5$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{tot}}{=}0.15$, $\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}{=}1.0$; $\tau{=}1$,
$k{=}4$; dead-zone $\rho{=}0.05$ where marked) so comparisons isolate the
update rule, not tuning effort.

Three observations. \textbf{(1) The update form beats backpropagation with
the same reward signal:} B and A optimize the same critic scalar
(\cref{prop:equiv}), yet B reaches $0.92$--$0.94$ on square where A
plateaus at $0.839$ \todo{confirm at matched 20K budget} --- the
action-space trust region is what permits the aggressive
$\eta_Q$ that backpropagation cannot survive. \textbf{(2) A drifting base
plus \method{} beats the stronger flow-matching base plus DICE-RL},
inverting the base-quality ordering ($0.877$ vs $0.957$ pre-RL on can).
\textbf{(3) The critic's gradient is worth trusting here:} the value-only
dial settings (C, T) preserve can but collapse on square
($\sim\!0.6$ vs $\sim\!0.93$) despite healthy residuals and longer
training --- with a critic in its easy regime, ranking-resolution reward
fields discard the signal that matters, consistent with the toy's easy
regime.
