\subsection{Setting}

We consider online fine-tuning of a generative robot policy pretrained from
demonstrations. An observation $s$ includes the inputs used by the policy,
and an action $a \in \mathbb{R}^{H d}$ is a chunk of $H$ controls with $d$
dimensions each. A frozen generator $\pi_0$ maps $s$ and noise $z$ to an
action. Following residual RL~\cite{dicerl2026}, we learn a correction:
\begin{equation}
  a_\theta(s,z)=\pi_0(s,z)+r_\theta(s,z),
  \qquad z\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I).
  \label{eq:residual}
\end{equation}
An ensemble critic estimates return from replayed experience. Reward is
provided on task success. The goal is to increase success through online
interaction while retaining useful pretrained behavior. Our controlled
comparison varies the actor update while matching the base, critic-training
procedure, and interaction budget.

\subsection{The Problem: Accumulated Departure}

A critic can assign high values to actions outside its training coverage.
Repeatedly following those predictions may move the policy away from actions
that actually succeed~\cite{fujimoto2019bcq}. A step cap alone does not
prevent this: $\|a_{t+1}-a_t\|\leq\delta$ permits distance from the initial
action to grow with the number of updates. Here $t$ indexes optimization
steps, not control steps. Fig.~\ref{fig:problem} compares two updates from
the same base distribution under the same critic. Vanilla ascent moves
probability mass into an overestimated region; anchoring each particle to
its own base action permits improvement nearby.

The design question is how to permit useful corrections without allowing
uncontrolled departure from the frozen base. We measure this departure by
the residual's root-mean-square action magnitude,
$D_{\mathrm{base}}=\mathbb{E}_{s,z}[\|r_\theta(s,z)\|_2/\sqrt{H d}]$,
with states sampled from replay. A fixed-base restoring term acts on this
departure, whereas step clipping limits only each proposed displacement.
We study both empirically: bounded regression targets do not themselves
guarantee bounded outputs after fitting a shared neural network.
